# INTERVUE-AI

AN AI CHATBOT THAT HELPS YOU TO PREPARE FOR INTERVIEWS BY PROVIDING ACCURATE SIMULATIONS OF A REAL INTERVIEW

##😎 Key Features

- 🔊 **Voice-Based Responses:** Speak your answers aloud during mock interviews with real-time speech-to-text integration.
- ⏱️ **Real-Time Timers:** Simulate actual time constraints per question.
- 📊 **Performance Analytics:** Get actionable feedback on your answers and delivery.

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. Select your target domain and role.
2. Listen to or read the interviewer's question.
3. Click **Record** and deliver your answer vocally.
4. Review your transcribed response and feedback.
