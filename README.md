# INTERVUE-AI

AN AI CHATBOT THAT HELPS YOU TO PREPARE FOR INTERVIEWS BY PROVIDING ACCURATE SIMULATIONS OF A REAL INTERVIEW

## 😎 Key Features

- 🔊 **Voice-Based Responses:** Speak your answers aloud during mock interviews with real-time speech-to-text integration.
- ⏱️ **Real-Time Timers:** Simulate actual time constraints per question.
- 📊 **Performance Analytics:** Get actionable feedback on your answers and delivery.

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. Select your target domain and role.
2. Listen to or read the interviewer's question.
3. Click **Record** and deliver your answer vocally.
4. Review your transcribed response and feedback.

## Steps to use:

1. Click on Start Mock Interview
2. Select the desired Core Topic & Role Alignment configuration
3. Modify the Interview Classification based on your ideal scenario
4. Make any required changes to the Constraints, Style & Mode option to suit your needs
5. Hit Enter Interview Room
6. Wait for the AI interviewer to start with the questions and then switch on your mic and answer them vocally or type out the answer based    on your comfort
7. You can conclude the interview or go through with it and you will get an analysis of your mock interview
8. You can check a cumulative assessment of all your mock interviews in the Dashboard to better understand the areas of improvement based      on your performance.
